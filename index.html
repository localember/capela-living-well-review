FAQ

Common Questions About Functional Medicine and Weight Loss

Answers to some of the questions we hear most from patients in Wooster.

Ask us directly
What happens at a first visit?
We take a full history and talk through what you are feeling and when it started before anything else happens. You will hear what we found and what we recommend before you decide on care.
Do I need a referral to come in?
No. You can book directly with our office.
How is this different from just going to my doctor?
It is not in competition with your doctor. A physician is looking for disease that needs medical management, and is very good at that. This looks at the space before that, where you feel unwell but nothing has crossed a diagnostic threshold, and it has the appointment length to go through your history properly. Plenty of our patients keep seeing both, and that is the right arrangement.
How long before I feel different?
Honestly, it varies, and anyone who gives you a number for this is guessing. Some people notice a difference in energy or digestion within a few weeks of changing the obvious things. Where something has been building for years, it takes longer. We set a review point at the start so there is a defined moment to look at whether this is working, rather than an open-ended commitment.
How much weight will I lose?
We do not give a number before meeting you, and we would be wary of anyone who does. It depends on where you are starting from, what your sleep and stress look like, and what you can realistically keep doing. After the first consultation we can talk honestly about what is reasonable for you.
