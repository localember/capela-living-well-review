The short answer

Supplements can be one part of your plan at Living Well. Our team helps you work out which ones fit you, and the Supplement Scan is one way to start that conversation.

Here is how supplements fit into care at Living Well, and how to ask about them.

Guided supplementation

Guided supplementation is part of our weight loss program, alongside red light therapy, Styku body scans and personalized nutrition. When a supplement is recommended, we explain why.

The Supplement Scan

The Supplement Scan uses a Zyto device and takes about 15 minutes. You rest your hand on a pad and chat with our team while it runs. It is painless. Afterward we go over the report with you and the supplements it points to.
